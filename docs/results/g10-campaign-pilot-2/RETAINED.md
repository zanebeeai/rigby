# Pilot 2

The second scored pass of the G10 protocol, retained whole: every row in `trials.json`, the summary, the provenance, the protocol as registered then (`protocol/`), and the rendered media of every failed, undecided or interrupted episode. The media of successful episodes and every replayable bundle live in the local results tree (`any-robot/results/g10-campaign-pilot-2`, untracked) and are named by digest in the rows.

It ran on the G06 fixtures standing on a table and planned each acquisition to the mean of the still frames, observing with the front camera alone; the scored pass observes with the front and the overhead cameras, and a required kind is decided from every usable sensor of it rather than refused when one of them is blind. See `docs/results/g10-report.md`.
