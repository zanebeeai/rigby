# Pilot 1

The first scored pass of the G10 protocol, retained whole: every row in `trials.json`, the summary, the provenance, the protocol as then registered (`protocol/`), and the rendered media of every failed, undecided or interrupted episode. The media of successful episodes and every replayable bundle live in the local results tree (`any-robot/results/g10-campaign-pilot-1`, untracked) and are named by digest in the rows.

It ran on the G06 fixtures over a floor alone and planned each acquisition to the newest camera frame; the scored pass stands the fixtures on a table and plans to the mean of the frames that showed the object still. See `docs/results/g10-report.md`.
